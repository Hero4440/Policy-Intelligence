# PolicyPilot Hackathon Video Script
**Duration:** 60 seconds  
**Format:** Screen recording + voiceover  
**Music:** Subtle, uplifting background (royalty-free)

---

## Scene Breakdown

### **SCENE 1: THE PROBLEM (0-10 seconds)**

#### What's on Screen:
- **0-3s:** Title card on dark background
  - Text: "Every day, clinic staff face a challenge"
  - Font: Large, white, centered
  - Music fades in (subtle, 30% volume)

- **3-7s:** Quick montage (3-4 seconds total):
  - Slide 1 (1.5s): Stock image of tired healthcare worker OR screenshot of cluttered desktop with multiple browser tabs open
  - Slide 2 (1.5s): Prescription denial letter (or simulated)
  - Text overlay: "Hours wasted. Patients delayed."

- **7-10s:** Text card appears:
  - "How do we verify insurance coverage fast?"
  - White text, centered

#### Voiceover Script (read slowly, clear):
> "Every day, clinic staff spend hours verifying if patients qualify for medications under their insurance. They dig through dense policy documents, check multiple payers, manually verify criteria. It's tedious. It's error-prone. And it delays care."

#### Tone:
- Problem is real, but not doom-and-gloom
- Conversational, relatable

---

### **SCENE 2A: DEMO PART 1 - DRUG COVERAGE SEARCH (10-25 seconds)**

#### What's on Screen:
- **10-12s:** PolicyPilot home screen appears
  - Highlight the main search bar with a glow/pulse animation
  - Text overlay: "Step 1: Compare Coverage"

- **12-18s:** Demo action:
  - User types "Humira" in search bar
  - Clicks "Compare Payers" button
  - **Compare table loads** showing 3 payers (Aetna, UHC, Cigna)
  - Columns visible: Drug Name | Payer | Status | PA Required | Restrictions
  - Color-coded badges: Green (Covered), Orange (PA Required), Red (Not Covered)
  - Highlight one cell (e.g., "PA Required" under Aetna) with a box outline

- **18-20s:** Click on highlighted cell
  - Evidence panel pops up on right side
  - Shows: Quote from policy + page number
  - Text overlay: "Evidence-backed instantly"

- **20-25s:** Pan out to full table
  - Text overlay: "All payers compared. All with sources."

#### Voiceover Script (read at natural pace):
> "Meet PolicyPilot. Start by searching for a drug. See coverage across all your payers instantly. Compare who covers it, who requires prior auth, and who has restrictions. Click any cell to see the exact policy text that backs it up."

#### Visual Cues:
- Use arrow or hand cursor to point to interactive elements
- Keep the comparison table on screen for full 15 seconds so viewers can read it

---

### **SCENE 2B: DEMO PART 2 - PATIENT EVALUATION (25-45 seconds)**

#### What's on Screen:
- **25-27s:** Transition to "Patient Cases" section
  - Text overlay: "Step 2: Check Patient Eligibility"
  - Show patient list with a case highlighted

- **27-30s:** Click on a patient case (e.g., "Sarah Martinez - Humira")
  - Case detail page loads
  - Show: Patient name, Payer, Drug, Diagnosis
  - Highlight "Documents" section

- **30-35s:** Upload document animation
  - Drag-and-drop a clinical note onto screen (simulated)
  - Progress bar: "Extracting patient facts..."
  - Facts appear: Diagnosis, Prior therapies, Prescriber type
  - Text overlay: "AI extracts facts automatically"

- **35-40s:** Click "Run Evaluation" button
  - Evaluation results load with status badge: **"PA REQUIRED"** (orange)
  - Below that: Checklist appears with items like:
    - ✅ Indication met (patient has RA)
    - ❌ Prior therapy documented (missing)
    - ✅ Appropriate diagnosis
  - Text overlay: "Checklist shows exactly what's needed"

- **40-45s:** Scroll down to show "Next Steps" section
  - Three columns appear:
    - **For Clinic:** "Send prior auth form with diagnoses"
    - **For Patient:** "Expect 3-5 day review"
    - **For Analyst:** "Request prior therapy records"
  - Text overlay: "Role-specific guidance"

#### Voiceover Script (read steadily):
> "Upload a patient's documents. The system extracts key facts automatically. Then run a coverage evaluation. You get a clear status—covered, prior auth required, or not eligible. And a checklist of exactly what criteria are met and what's missing. No guessing. Then get role-specific next steps for your clinic, the patient, and the payer analyst."

#### Visual Cues:
- Use smooth zoom on checklist items so text is readable
- Keep each step on screen long enough to read (3-5 seconds)

---

### **SCENE 3: BONUS FEATURE - CHAT (45-55 seconds)**

#### What's on Screen:
- **45-47s:** Quick transition to Chat section
  - Text overlay: "Bonus: AI-Powered Q&A"
  - Show empty chat interface

- **47-52s:** Demo chat interaction:
  - Message appears: "Does Sarah meet PA criteria for Humira under Aetna?"
  - Wait 1 second (show typing indicator)
  - AI response appears: "Based on Aetna's policy, Sarah meets the indication criteria (RA diagnosis documented). However, **prior therapy documentation is missing**. She needs evidence of prior therapy failure before approval. [See policy page 3]"
  - Highlight the citation link with a box
  - Text overlay: "All answers cited"

- **52-55s:** Click citation
  - Evidence snippet appears in sidebar showing the exact policy text
  - Text overlay: "Evidence always visible"

#### Voiceover Script (read conversationally):
> "Ask questions in natural language. Get answers backed by policy evidence instantly. All answers show their sources, so you always know where the information comes from."

#### Visual Cues:
- Keep chat response readable (increase font size if needed)
- Show the citation being clicked for 2-3 seconds

---

### **SCENE 4: CLOSE & IMPACT (55-60 seconds)**

#### What's on Screen:
- **55-57s:** Montage of quick wins (2 seconds):
  - Slide 1 (1s): Clock icon with "Hours → Minutes"
  - Slide 2 (1s): Checkmark icon with "Evidence-backed decisions"

- **57-59s:** Full-screen text card:
  - Large text: "PolicyPilot"
  - Subtext: "Intelligence. Evidence. Speed."
  - Background: Subtle gradient (cream to light teal)

- **59-60s:** Logo appears with tagline
  - Text: "Healthcare policy intelligence platform"
  - Small: "For clinics, analysts, and patients"
  - Fade out

#### Voiceover Script (read with confidence):
> "PolicyPilot: Intelligence, evidence, and speed for healthcare coverage decisions. Built for clinics that care."

#### Visual/Audio Cues:
- Music volume increases slightly at "PolicyPilot"
- Fade to black at 60s
- End credits (optional): "Made for [Hackathon Name]"

## Production Checklist

### Recording
- [ ] Record at 1080p or 4K
- [ ] Ensure all UI text is readable (test on small screen)
- [ ] Use smooth scrolling/transitions (no jarring cuts)
- [ ] Cursor is visible and slow enough to follow
- [ ] Click buttons clearly and pause 1 second to show result

### Voiceover
- [ ] Record in quiet room
- [ ] Speak clearly, not too fast (aim for 120-140 wpm)
- [ ] Match voiceover timing to on-screen action
- [ ] Use natural pauses between sentences
- [ ] Edit out mouth clicks and breathing noise

### Text Overlays
- [ ] All text white with dark shadow for contrast
- [ ] Font: Sans-serif (Arial, Inter, or similar), min 24pt
- [ ] Keep on screen for 3-5 seconds
- [ ] Center or top-left, don't cover UI elements

### Music
- [ ] Find royalty-free background: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or YouTube Audio Library
- [ ] Uplifting, modern, 60-80 BPM
- [ ] Volume: 20-30% during voiceover, 50% during transitions
- [ ] Fade in at 3s, fade out at 55s

### Final Video
- [ ] Total duration: 59-61 seconds
- [ ] Format: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- [ ] Resolution: 1920x1080 @ 30fps
- [ ] Audio: 128 kbps AAC, mono or stereo
- [ ] File size: < 50 MB

## Notes for Recording

1. **Use your actual app:** Screenshots from your live PolicyPilot, not mockups
2. **Test data:** Use the synthetic demo patients (Sarah Martinez, Jennifer Kim, etc.) to make it relatable
3. **Zoom into UI:** If text is small, use screen zoom (150-200%) so it's readable
4. **Smooth transitions:** Use fade or slide transitions between scenes (not jarring cuts)
5. **Cursor visibility:** Make sure your mouse cursor is visible and moves deliberately
6. **Realistic timing:** Don't make things load instantly; let progress bars show briefly so it feels authentic
