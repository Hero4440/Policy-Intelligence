<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Create deterministic patient-vs-policy evaluation domain</name>
  <files>src/storage/types.ts, src/server/patient-evaluation.ts</files>
  <action>
    Create a backend evaluation module that turns stored patient facts plus a selected policy version into a Phase 9 checklist.

    Requirements:
    - accept:
      - `caseId`
      - `policyId`
      - `policyVersion`
    - load the case and selected policy version from disk
    - build checklist items from policy criteria such as:
      - diagnosis requirements
      - prior auth requirements
      - step therapy
      - other requirements / restrictions
    - map deterministic matching outcomes into Phase 9 checklist labels:
      - `PASS`
      - `MISSING`
      - `UNKNOWN`
      - `NEEDS REVIEW`
    - every checklist item includes:
      - criterion text
      - patient-side matched fact reference when present
      - source policy evidence snippet/reference

    Status mapping:
    - `Covered`: checklist cleanly passes and policy is favorable without unmet PA-style blockers
    - `PA Required`: policy posture requires PA but available evidence otherwise supports proceeding
    - `Likely Eligible but Docs Missing`: some evidence suggests eligibility but at least one required criterion is missing documentation
    - `Not Covered`: policy posture or missing core criterion clearly blocks coverage
    - `Preferred Alternative Required`: policy explicitly prefers or requires another product path
    - `Unclear`: evidence is too incomplete or ambiguous to make a stronger claim

    Reuse existing matching utilities where helpful, but adapt the output to the exact Phase 9 language instead of leaking the older matcher vocabulary into the UI payload.
  </action>
  <verify>
    `npx tsc --noEmit` passes.
    At least one seeded or synthetic case can be evaluated end-to-end and returns coverage status plus checklist items.
  </verify>
  <done>
    Phase 9 has a deterministic evaluation engine that produces explainable checklist output.
  </done>
</task>
